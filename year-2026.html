A year in review · 2026
2026
The year I barely paused for breath, anchored, as ever, in Minsk, and it was autumnal October that burned brightest. 16 journeys in all, from Big weekend - winter SPB 🚌 in January to Turkey + Moldova + 3 more in September, 53 flights stitched the distances together, the furthest I got was Urgench, 3,368 km from home. 4 new countries opened up to me, 29 cities I'd never seen before — Istanbul and Moscow among them. I shared most of it with Andrei Patsiomkin. It began at Рождественская ярмарка / Калядны кiрмаш in January and ended at Прилуки in Pryluky. One note I left behind: “На закате :)”
