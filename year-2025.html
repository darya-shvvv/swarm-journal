A year in review · 2025
2025
The year I let familiar streets deepen instead of chasing new ones, with Minsk the place I kept returning to, and amber September ran fastest of the twelve. I travelled 19 times over, opening with Minsk - first visit 🚂 in February and closing with Hrodna - teeth therapy 🚂 in November, I took to the air 31 times, at the farthest I stood 913 km out, in Lobok. I set foot in Belarus for the first time, I met 29 new cities, Hrodna and Saint Petersburg among them. More often than not, Andrei Patsiomkin was there too. I opened the year at Grodno Railway Station (Чыгуначны вакзал) in Hrodna and signed it off in December at Зона Гостеприимства. A line I left on a check-in that year: “Nails time 💅”
