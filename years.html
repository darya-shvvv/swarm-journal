Year in review · 2 chapters

All your years, gathered.

Pick a year. Open the album. Let the photos do most of the talking.

From 2025 to 2026 — 2 years of checking in, 8,510 times over, a habit I never quite meant to keep and never managed to break. They add up to 6 countries and 58 cities, and something like 51,306 km of getting there. Minsk is the name that keeps recurring — the place I kept coming back to between everywhere else. Andrei Patsiomkin turns up across a lot of these years; the best of them usually did. The farthest I ever got was Urgench, about 3,368 km from home. Each card below is one year of it. Start wherever you like.

The year I barely paused for breath, anchored, as ever, in Minsk, and it was autumnal October that burned brightest. 17 journeys in all, from Big weekend - winter SPB 🚌 in January to Pryluky, Belarus in October, 53 flights stitched the distances together, the furthest I got was Urgench, 3,368 km from home. 4 new countries opened up to me, 29 cities I'd never seen before — Istanbul and Moscow among them. I shared most of it with Andrei Patsiomkin. It began at Рождественская ярмарка / Калядны кiрмаш in January and ended at Комсомольская улица. One note I left behind: “На закате :)”

The year I let familiar streets deepen instead of chasing new ones, with Minsk the place I kept returning to, and amber September ran fastest of the twelve. I travelled 19 times over, opening with Minsk - first visit 🚂 in February and closing with Hrodna - teeth therapy 🚂 in November, I took to the air 31 times, at the farthest I stood 913 km out, in Lobok. I set foot in Belarus for the first time, I met 29 new cities, Hrodna and Saint Petersburg among them. More often than not, Andrei Patsiomkin was there too. I opened the year at Grodno Railway Station (Чыгуначны вакзал) in Hrodna and signed it off in December at Зона Гостеприимства. A line I left on a check-in that year: “Nails time 💅”
